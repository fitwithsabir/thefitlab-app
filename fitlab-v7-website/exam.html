Exam rules — read before you start
  • One question at a time, 60 seconds each. When the time runs out the paper moves on and that question counts as wrong.
  • No going back. Once you answer, the next question appears — you can't return to earlier ones.
  • Stay on this page. Switching tabs or apps, minimising, leaving full screen, reloading or closing the page cancels the paper. You then start again from Question 1 with new questions.
  • 3 cancelled papers in 24 hours lock the exam for 24 hours. Cancelled papers don't use up your exam attempts.
  • Your own knowledge only. No notes, other people, phones or AI tools. Questions test knowledge and coaching decisions from all six modules — they are not the checks from inside the lessons — and they and their options are shuffled for every paper. Every paper is marked with your name and ID.
  • Before you start: close other apps, silence notifications, charge your device and use a steady connection.
