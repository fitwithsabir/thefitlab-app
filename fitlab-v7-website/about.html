About us

The Fit Lab

The Fit Lab is a professional network, job board and academy for people who work in health and fitness in India — coaches, trainers, nutritionists, physiotherapists, gyms, studios and clinics.

It was started by Sabir Rasool, a head coach in Kakinada, Andhra Pradesh, to give fitness professionals one place to learn, get certified, show their work, find jobs and find clients.

What we offer

  • The Fit Lab Academy. Online courses for fitness coaches with a timed exam and a certificate anyone can check by QR code. The 30-Day Fitness Coaching Mastery course costs ₹99, or you can take the certification exam directly for ₹99.
  • The network. A free profile, posts, connections and messages with other people in the industry.
  • Jobs. Gyms, studios and clinics post jobs with the pay shown, and members apply for free.
  • Premium. Paid plans — Pro, Business and Recruiter — for members who want to get hired faster, grow their practice or hire people. Paid one month or one year at a time; nothing renews automatically.
  • Coaching bookings. Members can book sessions with coaches and pay online.

All prices are on our pricing page. Payments are taken securely by Razorpay.
