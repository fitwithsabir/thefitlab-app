Questions

Before you join

What does ₹99 include?

Everything: the course (or direct exam entry), 3 exam attempts, your certificate and your public verification page. No hidden fees and nothing renews.

Is it fully online?

Yes — on your phone or laptop. Lessons are in English and take about 25 minutes a day. Access never expires.

Full course or direct certification — which should I pick?

If you train clients already and know your basics, go direct and take the exam now. If you're new or want structure, take the full course — the exam comes after Day 30. Both cost ₹99 and lead to the same certificate.

How is the exam kept fair?

Questions come one at a time with 60 seconds each, the order and options are shuffled for every person, and you can't go back. Switching apps or tabs, leaving full screen or closing the page cancels the paper — you start again with new questions. Three cancelled papers in a day lock the exam for 24 hours. Cancelled papers don't use your 3 attempts.

Is the certificate recognised?

It is The Fit Lab Academy's own certificate. Each one has a QR code and your Fit Lab ID, so anyone — a gym, a client — can check it's genuine in seconds. It is not a government or university qualification.

What if I don't pass?

You get 3 attempts. Study the chapters you found hard, then try again when you're ready.

Refunds?

Payments aren't refundable except where the law requires or a payment was taken in error — see our terms. Write to hello@thefitlab.in and we'll look at every request.
